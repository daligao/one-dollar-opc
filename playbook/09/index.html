Lesson 09 of 12

Portfolio Management

You Will End Up With Multiple Projects

Most one-person companies start with one website. Six months later, they have four. A year later, they have eight.

Not all of them deserve the same level of attention. Some are growing. Some are dead. Some are experiments that have not yet shown what they are.

Portfolio management is the skill of deciding which one gets your time this week.

The Four Buckets

Grow

Working. Has traction. Deserves your best time and effort right now.

Maintain

Generating income. Stable. Needs only occasional updates and monitoring.

Experiment

New. Not enough data yet. Give it a defined window, then decide.

Retire

No traction, no revenue, no path forward. Archive it. Don't pretend.

How to Score a Project

Monthly, ask these questions about each active project:

  • Is it getting organic traffic? (Growing / Flat / Declining)
  • Is it generating any revenue? (Yes / No)
  • Have I improved it in the last 30 days? (Yes / No)
  • Does it have a realistic path to revenue in the next 90 days? (Yes / No)

If the answer to all four is No, it is a Retire candidate. Don't keep paying for a domain you're not working on.

Capital Allocation

Your capital is not just money. It is also time and attention.

Grow bucket: 70% of new effort Maintain bucket: 20% (monitoring, updates) Experiment: 10% (one new thing at a time)

If you are constantly moving between six active projects with no clear priority, nothing will grow fast enough to matter.

"The most important OPC skill is not doing more. It's knowing where to focus limited resources."
